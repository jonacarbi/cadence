Voice: plain, specific, no hype. No em dashes. Safety claims always say reduces risk, no guarantee.

Regenerate PNG and ICNS from the SVG with rsvg-convert, ImageMagick and iconutil. The generator script was kept outside the repo; the SVGs are the source.
